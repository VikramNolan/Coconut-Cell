ABOUT ECO-SHELL

A useful second life for every shell.

Our story starts with discarded coconut shells — a familiar agricultural by-product with warmth, texture and practical strength. We work to turn this overlooked material into useful kitchenware and fashion accessories that feel good to use every day.

Our mission

Make sustainable kitchenware and fashion accessories accessible and affordable while reducing coconut waste.

Our vision

A future where agricultural waste becomes valuable, beautiful, everyday products.

Why coconut shells?

Strong natural material
Upcycled waste
Reusable for everyday rituals
Distinctive natural texture

HOW WE MAKE IT

From shell to shelf, one
careful step at a time.

A simple making process preserves the material's natural character while preparing it for practical, everyday use.

Collect

Gather discarded shells for a new purpose.

Clean

Prepare each shell with care.

Dry

Allow the natural material to dry fully.

Shape

Form each piece for its intended use.

Sand

Smooth surfaces while keeping their character.

Add Lid

Add practical details where needed.

Finish & Test

Check the final piece for everyday readiness.

Package

Prepare it for its next home.

SUSTAINABILITY, PLAINLY

Making more of what
already exists.

ECO-SHELL turns discarded coconut shell waste into useful, reusable, attractive products. By extending the life of this natural material through mindful production and durable everyday objects, we aim to make lower-waste choices feel practical and beautiful.

We do not claim perfection. We simply believe a useful material deserves more than one life.
